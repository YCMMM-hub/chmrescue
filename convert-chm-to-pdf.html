Convert CHM to PDF

There are free desktop tools for this (Calibre can convert CHM files), and online converters that require uploading your file. CHMRescue offers a third route that stays on your computer.

How CHMRescue does it

  1. Open the manual in CHMRescue and click Export… (Export Pass).
  2. Choose a part (large manuals are split into parts of 400 pages), click Assemble: a cover page, a contents list and every page are put into one document.
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF as the destination in your browser’s print window.

What to expect: this is not a one-click converter — your browser’s print window opens. Text, images, tables and code are kept; the original page styling of the manual is simplified. Please check an important document after conversion.

Open it in your browser

Drop the file into CHMRescue: contents, search and images work offline, and the file is never uploaded. Reading is free.

Open a CHM file

Limits, honestly: encrypted CHM files do not open, and some Free Pascal manuals (duplicate directory entries) are refused with a clear message. Search is a plain-text search of the pages. Old scripts inside CHM pages do not run — on purpose, for your safety.

Questions

Why not a one-click PDF?

To stay permission-free and offline, CHMRescue uses your browser’s own print engine rather than a server or a special permission.

Is there a free way?

Yes: desktop tools such as Calibre, or reading the manual free in CHMRescue and printing single pages.
