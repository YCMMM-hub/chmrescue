What you get

  • Table of contents, page navigation, previous / next.
  • Full-text search across the manual.
  • Images and styles from the manual, shown as they were authored.
  • Works offline once the page or extension is loaded.

What it does not do

It is a reader and an exporter, not an editor. See the limits below.

Limits, honestly: encrypted CHM files do not open, and some Free Pascal manuals (duplicate directory entries) are refused with a clear message. Search is a plain-text search of the pages. Old scripts inside CHM pages do not run — on purpose, for your safety.

Questions

Is it really not uploaded?

The page makes no request to any server when you open, search or export a file. You can check it yourself with your browser’s developer tools (Network tab).

Which browsers?

Built and tested in Chrome. Other modern browsers may work; they have not been tested yet.
