Privacy policy / Confidentialité

Applies to the CHMRescue website and the CHMRescue browser extension.

Your files

Your CHM files are read inside your browser, on your own computer. They are never uploaded, stored or seen by us. Opening, searching and exporting make no network request. / Vos fichiers CHM sont lus dans votre navigateur, sur votre ordinateur : jamais envoyés, jamais stockés, jamais vus par nous.

Data we collect

The extension collects no data at all: no analytics, no cookies, no account, no tracking. The only thing it stores on your device is, if you buy the Export Pass and enter your code, your activation code (so you do not have to enter it again). It never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the extension.

Payment

Payment is optional and happens outside the extension, on a payment page opened in a new tab and operated by a payment processor under its own privacy policy. We receive the e-mail address and name you give at checkout, only to send your activation code and answer support requests. We never receive your card details.
